## Implemented architecture and configuration
- `Resources/RVFuelComponent`: replicated authority-only litre tank; gasoline/diesel/propane Gameplay Tags; configurable capacity and initial amount; bounded add/burn and RepNotify/delegate.
- `Resources/RVBatteryComponent`: reusable house/chassis Wh storage with W charge/discharge ceilings, finite input checks and replicated energy.
- `Resources/RVEnergyProfile`: Primary Data Asset with normal fuel rates, source ordering, generator source/reserve, battery-facing rates and domain/priority load definitions.
- `Resources/RVElectricalComponent`: centralized authoritative power bus. Enabled loads receive source energy in configured priority before battery fallback; unused generated energy charges once, subject to battery limits. Chassis alternator supply is separate. Exhaustion accounts for a final fractional interval without claiming full powered status. No custom network transport or effect scheduler.
- `Vehicles/RVSystemsMotorhome`: composition subclass of the accepted interior motorhome. Actual Chaos throttle/velocity and driver/idle state drive resource integration. Empty fuel changes supported engine torque to zero; refueling restores the original configured torque. Brakes, steering, suspension, transmission, seat ownership and possession are retained. Two visible representative interior point lights follow replicated load state.
- `Vehicles/RVResourcePoint`: accepted held interaction selects a reachable fuel-fill or power endpoint. The original Power station opens the real panel on the Gate 6 derivative. Navigation optionally requires `PoweredStation`; hand-tool workbench, ordinary storage and other placeholders remain usable without that requirement.
- Accepted `RVItemDefinition`/`RVItemInstance`/`RVInventoryComponent` gain typed fuel metadata and per-instance litres/initialization/revision. Individual nonstackable cans retain GUID, condition, quantity and custom state through whole-record transfers/drop/repick. Fuel initializes once; transfer never refills a can. Partial refuel serially moves only available tank capacity, subtracts the identical amount, and advances the can revision. Empty hardware remains an item.
- Owned controller reliable RPCs validate current selected endpoint, 300 cm eye distance, Visibility LOS, owned GUID, compatible tag, amount/capacity and revision; refueling also requires RV speed <=150 cm/s. No client amount is accepted. Panel requests carry desired state and expected shared revision, with a 0.1 s request gate. There is no exclusive operator lock; generator state survives an operator disconnect.
- Native Enhanced Input Started bindings on the controller: H lights, J generator, K refrigerator, L powered equipment, O parked engine idle, Escape close panel. Hold E opens the endpoint; I opens inventory, Tab selects items and F uses the selected fuel can at the fill. Seated fuel/battery monitor is read-only until a valid panel is selected. Presentation reads authoritative replication; UMG survival HUD remains separate and unchanged.

Units: litres, watt-hours, watts, litres/hour, simulation seconds centrally converted to hours. Normal prototype: main 200/150 L; optional separate generator 30/20 L; shared-main generator pickup reserve 20 L. Idle burn 1.5 L/h, throttle contribution up to 14 L/h and road contribution 4 L/h at 2682.24 cm/s (60 mph), capped at twice that speed contribution. These are configurable representative rates, not final measured fuel economy. House 2400/1800 Wh, 800 W charging /1500 W discharging; chassis 800/600 Wh with 250 W charging. Generator 1800 W at 1.2 L/h; alternator 500 W house plus 250 W chassis; solar peak 300 W times available scalar, default zero; external/shore input 2000 W. Normal priority shore, generator, alternator, solar, then battery; configurable nonbattery order. Loads: lights 24 W, refrigerator 90 W, powered equipment 120 W. Chassis-domain loads are supported without adding headlights/starting failures.

Generator uses configured shared or separate tank, consumes only usable fuel and stops at exhaustion/reserve. Running/noise-strength query and change delegate prepare later encounter/AI integration; no attraction or final audio is implemented. Solar is an authoritative availability abstraction, not always-full weather simulation. External power is an authority-only hookup extension; no campground infrastructure. Refrigerator powered state/delegate prepare spoilage; no spoilage implemented.

Development tools require authority AND `rv.Resources.AllowDebug 1`, default off, rejected in Shipping. Reflected setters cover tank/can/battery/solar/time scale; bounded accelerated advance uses <=1 s integration steps and at most 86400 simulated seconds per call. Normal saved Data Asset rates are separate. Stable item GUIDs and scalar/configuration structs prepare future snapshots; full persistent vehicle identity, SaveGame loading, offline progression and region streaming are not implemented.

## Known limits and extension points
- Two-player listen-server PIE on this workstation, including separate processes and representative outgoing emulation. Packaged builds, dedicated servers, more players and arbitrary WAN conditions remain unvalidated.
- Flat-road prototype passenger/collision handling; rollovers, airborne passengers, rough terrain and larger standing parties remain outside the accepted test envelope. Canonical basing expects tagged floor pieces rigidly attached to one primitive attachment root; this RV uses its skeletal mesh root. Other moving-platform layouts should preserve that contract.
- Ideal Wh/W accounting, provisional normal rates and static on/off appliance demand. No battery chemistry/degradation, AC/DC wiring/inverter losses, starter/no-start simulation or mechanical faults.
- Driver presence automatically operates the prototype engine; O requests parked idle. Chassis battery is a charge/draw foundation, not a complete starter/ignition system. Future torque/efficiency modifiers should compose at the centralized vehicle/resource boundaries.
- Normal generator uses main gasoline with reserve. Separate tank burn/stop configuration is tested, but a dedicated separate-tank refueling UI is not provided. Noise is a query/delegate hook, not final audio or AI attraction.
- Solar input is a configurable authoritative scalar (normal zero); no weather/day-night model. Shore power is a server integration hook, not campground infrastructure.
- Prototype panel/markers/lights, closed exterior doors and fixed can weight/volume. Fuel density, varying carried mass and Chaos payload are not simulated. No final art/UI polish.
- Full SaveGame/load, persistent vehicle identity, reconnect inventory restoration, offline progression and seamless streaming remain future work. Item GUIDs and typed scalar/configuration state are prepared for serialization.
- No cooking/spoilage, mechanical failure/repair, medical effects, combat, AI or Gate 7 feature work.
